# Alchemist's Apotheosis

**Tagline:** *Master Forgotten Recipes. Brew Legendary Power.*

## Overview

**Alchemist's Apotheosis** is a strategy card game for **2–6 players**. Players gather magical ingredients, rediscover forgotten recipes, and brew increasingly powerful potions to earn **Renown**.

Every brewed potion:

- Immediately awards its printed Renown.
- May later be activated once for its magical effect.
- Continues contributing Renown after its Activated Effect has been used.

The player with the highest total Renown at the end of the game wins.

---

## Components

- **2–6 Alchemist's Apotheosis player boards**
- **36 Ingredient Cards**
- **18 Potion Cards**
  - 9 Apprentice Potions
  - 6 Master Potions
  - 3 Legendary Potions
- **Ingredient Market** — shared face-up Ingredient display
- **Potion Market** — shared face-up Potion display

The Ingredient Deck and Potion piles are formed from the corresponding cards during setup; they are not separate components.

---

## Setup

1. Shuffle all **36 Ingredient Cards** to form the Ingredient Deck.
2. Reveal **9 Ingredient Cards** face up to create the Ingredient Market.
3. Separate the Potion Cards into three piles:
   - **Apprentice Potions** — no tier symbol
   - **Master Potions** — marked **M**
   - **Legendary Potions** — marked **L**
4. Shuffle each Potion pile separately.
5. Create the Potion Market by revealing:
   - **4 Apprentice Potions**
   - **2 Master Potions**
   - **1 Legendary Potion**
6. Each player receives:
   - 1 Alchemist's Apotheosis player board
   - 3 Ingredient Cards
7. Randomly determine the first player.
8. Play proceeds clockwise.

---

## Goal of the Game

Brew potions to earn Renown.

A brewed potion immediately awards its printed Renown. It remains available for its Activated Effect until that effect is used. After activation, the potion still counts toward the player's final Renown total.

The player with the highest total Renown wins.

---

## Alchemist's Apotheosis Player Board

Each player board contains the following areas.

### Ingredient Rack

Stores ingredients currently available to the player for brewing.

The board provides **7 Ingredient slots** around the Cauldron.

### Cauldron

Ingredients selected during a Brew action are placed here temporarily while the brew is resolved.

### Potion Shelf

Stores up to **4 brewed potions** whose Activated Effects have not yet been used.

A player may never have more than four unactivated potions on the Potion Shelf.

### Completed Potions Area

After a potion's Activated Effect is used, move it from the Potion Shelf to the Completed Potions Area.

Completed Potions:

- Cannot be activated again.
- Continue contributing their printed Renown.

---

## Ingredient Cards

Every Ingredient Card contains:

- Ingredient Name
- Ingredient Type
- Element
- Two or three Alchemical Properties

When an ingredient is used in a brew, it contributes **all of its printed Alchemical Properties**.

A single ingredient may simultaneously contribute toward:

- Alchemical Property requirements
- One Element constraint
- One Ingredient Type constraint

### Ingredient Types

There are six Ingredient Types:

1. Herb
2. Crystal
3. Mushroom
4. Essence
5. Mineral
6. Animal Part

### Elements

Every ingredient belongs to one of six Elements:

1. Fire
2. Water
3. Earth
4. Air
5. Light
6. Shadow

### Alchemical Properties

There are six Alchemical Properties:

1. Enchanted
2. Explosive
3. Healing
4. Pure
5. Toxic
6. Volatile

Every Ingredient Card provides two or three Alchemical Properties.

---

## Potion Cards

Every Potion Card contains:

- Potion Name
- Tier
- Renown
- Recipe Requirements
- Recipe Constraints, if any
- Activated Effect

### Potion Tiers

- **Apprentice** — no tier symbol
- **Master** — marked **M**
- **Legendary** — marked **L**

Potion tier determines which Potion pile is used to refill its market position after the potion is brewed.

---

## Turn Structure

Starting with the first player and proceeding clockwise, each player performs **one** of the following actions on their turn:

1. **Gather**
2. **Brew**

---

## Gather

To Gather:

1. Choose and take **1 Ingredient Card** from the Ingredient Market.
2. Add it to your Ingredient Rack.
3. If the Ingredient Deck still contains cards, immediately reveal a replacement Ingredient Card into the empty market position.
4. If the Ingredient Deck is empty, do not replace the card. The Ingredient Market becomes smaller.
5. End your turn.

---

## Brew

To Brew:

1. Choose **1 Potion Card** from the Potion Market that you can legally brew.
2. You may optionally activate **1 potion** from your Potion Shelf.
3. Resolve that Activated Effect as applicable to the current brew.
4. Select the Ingredient Cards you will use and place them in your Cauldron.
5. Verify that all of the potion's **Recipe Requirements** are satisfied.
6. Verify that all of the potion's **Recipe Constraints** are satisfied.
7. Discard all ingredients used in the brew unless an Activated Effect specifically says otherwise.
8. Gain the brewed potion's printed **Renown immediately**.
9. Place the brewed Potion Card into an empty slot on your Potion Shelf.
10. Refill the empty Potion Market position from the **same tier** as the brewed potion, if a card remains in that pile.
11. End your turn.

### Potion Market Refill

Potion Market positions retain their tier:

- Apprentice replaces Apprentice.
- Master replaces Master.
- Legendary replaces Legendary.

If the corresponding Potion pile is empty, that market position remains empty.

---

## Brewing Rules

A potion may only be brewed if both its **Recipe Requirements** and **Recipe Constraints** are satisfied.

### Recipe Requirements

Recipe Requirements consist only of **Alchemical Properties**.

Every listed Property must be supplied by the ingredients used in the brew.

If a Property appears multiple times, that Property must be supplied the specified number of times.

#### Example

A recipe requiring:

> Healing ×2, Pure

requires the ingredients used in the brew to collectively provide:

- 2 Healing Properties
- 1 Pure Property

### Recipe Constraints

Recipe Constraints may require specific **Ingredient Types** or **Elements**.

Examples:

- **At least 2 Crystals** — at least two ingredients used in the brew must have the Crystal Ingredient Type.
- **At least 1 Fire ingredient** — at least one ingredient used in the brew must have the Fire Element.

Recipe Constraints are checked after confirming that all Alchemical Property requirements are satisfied.

### Excess Properties

Properties supplied beyond the recipe's requirements have no effect unless an Activated Effect specifically uses them.

---

## Activated Effects

Each brewed potion may be activated **once**.

An Activated Effect is used as part of a Brew action, before the ingredients for that brew are finalized.

Rules for activation:

- Activating a potion is optional.
- A maximum of **1 Activated Effect** may be used during a single Brew action.
- Resolve the effect for the current brew according to the Potion Card.
- After resolving the effect, move the activated potion from the Potion Shelf to the Completed Potions Area.
- The potion can never be activated again.
- The potion continues contributing its printed Renown.

---

## Potion Effect Glossary

### Infuse

Choose an ingredient used in the current brew. It gains the specified Alchemical Property for this brew only.

An ingredient may receive multiple infused Properties if the effect allows it.

### Attune

Choose an ingredient. Treat it as having **any Element** for the current brew.

### Disguise

Choose an ingredient. Treat it as **any Ingredient Type** for the current brew.

### Recover

Choose one ingredient used in the current brew. Instead of discarding it, return it to your Ingredient Rack after the brew.

### Stabilize

The chosen ingredients used in the current brew are not discarded. Return them to your Ingredient Rack after brewing.

### Conjure

Draw the specified number of Ingredient Cards from the Ingredient Deck and place them into your Ingredient Rack.

If the Ingredient Deck runs out before the full number can be drawn, choose the remaining required Ingredient Cards from the Ingredient Market instead.

### Ignore Element Requirements

Ignore all Element constraints on the current potion recipe.

Ingredient Type constraints must still be satisfied.

---

## Potion Shelf Limit

The Potion Shelf can hold a maximum of **4 unactivated potions**.

If all four Potion Shelf slots are occupied, the player cannot complete another Brew unless an existing potion is activated during that Brew, thereby freeing a Potion Shelf slot for the newly brewed potion.

---

## End of the Game

The game approaches its end when either **Potion Exhaustion** or **Ingredient Exhaustion** occurs.

### Potion Exhaustion

Potion Exhaustion occurs when:

- All three Potion piles are empty, **and**
- No Potion Cards remain in the Potion Market.

When no potions remain available to brew, the game ends.

### Ingredient Exhaustion

Ingredient Exhaustion occurs when:

- The Ingredient Deck is empty, **and**
- No Ingredient Cards remain in the Ingredient Market.

Players continue taking turns during Ingredient Exhaustion, but may only brew potions using ingredients already in their Ingredient Racks.

A player who cannot or chooses not to brew passes.

Players may continue brewing as long as they are able to do so.

The game ends immediately when **every player passes consecutively without any player brewing a potion**.

---

## Final Scoring

Each player totals the printed Renown on all of their brewed potions, including:

- Potions remaining on their Potion Shelf
- Potions in their Completed Potions Area

The player with the **highest total Renown** wins.

If two or more players are tied for the highest Renown, the victory is shared.

---

## Quick Reference

### Gather

1. Take 1 Ingredient Card from the Ingredient Market.
2. Add it to your Ingredient Rack.
3. Refill the market if the Ingredient Deck contains cards.
4. End turn.

### Brew

1. Choose a brewable Potion from the Potion Market.
2. Optionally activate 1 Potion from your Potion Shelf.
3. Select ingredients and place them in the Cauldron.
4. Satisfy all Alchemical Property requirements.
5. Satisfy all Ingredient Type and Element constraints.
6. Discard brewed ingredients unless an effect says otherwise.
7. Gain the Potion's Renown immediately.
8. Place the Potion on your Potion Shelf.
9. Refill its market position from the matching Potion tier, if possible.
10. End turn.

## Rules Interpretation Priorities for an AI Agent

When answering questions about **Alchemist's Apotheosis**, apply the following principles:

1. **Recipe Requirements are always Alchemical Properties.** Ingredient Types and Elements are constraints, not Property requirements.
2. **Ingredients contribute all printed Properties simultaneously.**
3. **One ingredient can satisfy Property, Type, and Element conditions at the same time.**
4. **Repeated Property requirements require repeated Property occurrences.**
5. **Only one potion may be activated per Brew action.**
6. **Activated potions continue to score Renown.**
7. **Used ingredients are discarded by default.** Recover, Stabilize, or another explicit potion effect can override this rule.
8. **Potion Market slots refill only from their matching tier.** An empty tier pile leaves that market slot empty.
9. **The Potion Shelf holds at most four unactivated potions.** Activating a potion during a Brew may free the slot needed for the newly brewed potion.
10. **Conjure uses the Ingredient Deck first.** Only if the deck runs out are remaining ingredients selected from the Ingredient Market.
11. **During Ingredient Exhaustion, Gather is no longer possible.** Players brew from their existing Ingredient Racks or pass.
12. **Final Renown includes both unactivated and activated/completed potions.**

## Canonical Terminology

Use these terms consistently when answering game questions:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ingredient Rack | Player's available Ingredient Cards |
| Cauldron | Temporary area for ingredients used in the current Brew |
| Potion Shelf | Up to four brewed potions whose effects have not been activated |
| Completed Potions Area | Brewed potions whose Activated Effects have already been used |
| Ingredient Market | Shared face-up Ingredient Cards |
| Potion Market | Shared face-up available Potion Cards |
| Recipe Requirements | Alchemical Properties required to brew a potion |
| Recipe Constraints | Ingredient Type and/or Element conditions on a recipe |
| Renown | Victory points earned from brewed potions |
