# Realmweaver: Hexbound — Rules Reference

**Tagline:** *Where Every Legend Finds Its Place.*

## Purpose of This Document

This document is the authoritative rules reference for answering questions about **Realmweaver: Hexbound**. When interpreting a question, prefer the explicit rules in this document over assumptions based on other board games.

---

## 1. Game Overview

**Realmweaver: Hexbound** is a family strategy game for **2–6 players**.

Players recruit legendary creatures from myth and folklore and place them into magical sanctuaries. Each creature has preferred habitats, attributes, and abilities that interact with the positions and characteristics of other creatures in the same sanctuary.

Strategy comes from two decisions:

1. Choosing an appropriate creature for the active sanctuary.
2. Choosing the best available position within that sanctuary.

Players score creatures as they are played. After every player has played all six creature cards from their hand, the player with the highest total score wins.

---

## 2. Components

- 1 Game Board
- 36 Creature Cards
- 36 Creature Icon Cards / Tokens
- 1 six-sided die (d6)
- 1 Score Pad

The game board contains **six sanctuaries**, and each sanctuary contains **six creature spaces arranged in a circle**.

---

## 3. Sanctuaries / Habitats

There are six habitats:

1. Forest
2. Mountain
3. Water
4. Sky
5. Ruins
6. Plains

Each habitat corresponds to one sanctuary on the board.

Each sanctuary contains six positions arranged in a circle. Position within this circle matters because creature abilities can refer to creatures that are adjacent, opposite, before, or after another creature.

---

## 4. Creature Families

There are six creature families, with six creatures in each family:

1. Celestials
2. Dragonoids
3. Beasts
4. Spirits
5. Monsters
6. Fey

Each family has a unique family icon printed on its creature cards.

Family identity may be referenced by creature abilities. For example, an ability may award points for being positioned before a Dragonoid or impose a penalty for being positioned after a Monster.

---

## 5. Creature Cards

Each Creature Card contains the information required to place and score that creature:

- Creature Name
- Family / Family Icon
- Illustration
- Base Points
- Home Habitat
- Roaming Habitat(s)
- Attributes
- Ability Name
- Ability Effect(s)

### Base Points

Base Points are the creature's starting score before habitat bonuses and ability modifiers are applied.

### Home Habitat

Every creature has exactly **one Home Habitat**.

The Home Habitat has a printed point bonus. If the creature is played in its Home Habitat, add that Home bonus to its score.

Example:

`Home: Sky (+3)`

A creature played in the Sky sanctuary receives +3 points from its Home Habitat.

### Roaming Habitats

Every creature has **one to three Roaming Habitats**.

Each Roaming Habitat has its own printed bonus, which may be positive or zero.

Example:

`Roaming: Mountain (+1), Ruins (+1)`

If the creature is played in Mountain, it receives +1. If played in Ruins, it receives +1.

### Legal Habitat Rule

A creature may only be played in:

- its Home Habitat, or
- one of its listed Roaming Habitats.

A creature cannot be played in an unlisted habitat.

---

## 6. Attributes

There are six attributes:

- Might
- Magic
- Flight
- Mysticism
- Fortitude
- Cunning

Each creature has two or three attributes.

Attributes do not independently provide points. They are used only when an ability specifically references them.

Example:

`+2 if adjacent to a creature with Flight.`

The bonus applies if the required positional relationship exists with a creature whose card lists Flight as an attribute.

---

## 7. Setup

1. Place the game board in the center of the table.
2. Shuffle all 36 Creature Cards together into one deck.
3. Deal **6 Creature Cards to each player**.
4. Choose a starting player.
5. Keep the Creature Icon Cards / Tokens accessible.
6. Keep the six-sided die and score pad nearby.

Each player's six dealt cards are the creatures that player will play during the game.

---

## 8. Round Sequence

Each round follows this sequence.

### Step 1 — Roll the Die

The active player rolls the six-sided die.

The die result determines the **active sanctuary**.

Each die result corresponds to one of the six sanctuaries.

### Step 2 — Check Sanctuary Capacity

The selected sanctuary can activate only if it contains enough empty spaces for **every player to place one creature during the round**.

If there are not enough empty spaces, roll again until an eligible sanctuary is selected.

Example:

In a four-player game, the active sanctuary must have at least four empty spaces. A sanctuary with only three empty spaces is not eligible and the die must be rolled again.

### Step 3 — Place Creatures

Starting with the active player and continuing clockwise, every player takes one placement turn in the active sanctuary.

On a placement turn:

1. Choose a Creature Card from your hand that may legally be played in the active sanctuary.
2. Place that creature's corresponding Creature Icon Card / Token into any empty position in the active sanctuary.
3. Keep the full Creature Card beside the sanctuary so its information remains visible for scoring and future ability interactions.

The exact position chosen matters because creature abilities may depend on other creatures being adjacent, opposite, before, or after the creature.

### Step 4 — Score the Creature

Score each creature **when it is played**.

Calculate:

**Creature Score = Base Points + Habitat Bonus + Applicable Ability Modifiers**

The Habitat Bonus is either:

- the creature's Home bonus, if placed in its Home Habitat, or
- the listed Roaming bonus for the active habitat.

Ability modifiers include all positive and negative effects that apply based on the board state at the moment the creature is scored.

Record the resulting score on the score pad.

### Current Board State Rule

A creature's score is based on the **current board state when that creature is played**.

Do not retroactively rescore previously played creatures when later creatures are added.

### Step 5 — Pass the Starting Player

After all players have placed and scored one creature, the starting-player role passes clockwise.

The next round begins with the new active player rolling the die.

## 9. Position Relationships

All positional relationships are evaluated **within the same sanctuary**.

The six spaces form a circular arrangement.

### Adjacent

**Adjacent means either Before or After.**

The two positions immediately next to a creature are adjacent to it:

- the position immediately counter-clockwise (**Before**), and
- the position immediately clockwise (**After**).

Therefore, an ability that says:

`+2 if adjacent to a creature with Flight`

can be satisfied by a qualifying creature in either the Before position or the After position.

### Opposite

The position directly across the circle from a creature is **Opposite**.

With six spaces in a sanctuary, every position has exactly one opposite position.

### Before

**Before = the immediately counter-clockwise position.**

Only the single space immediately counter-clockwise counts as Before unless a card explicitly states otherwise.

### After

**After = the immediately clockwise position.**

Only the single space immediately clockwise counts as After unless a card explicitly states otherwise.

### Relationship Summary

For a creature occupying one of the six sanctuary positions:

- **Before:** immediately counter-clockwise
- **After:** immediately clockwise
- **Adjacent:** Before OR After
- **Opposite:** directly across the sanctuary

Empty spaces do not satisfy creature-based ability conditions.

---

## 10. Creature Abilities

Creature abilities are written in plain English on the cards.

Abilities may reference:

- Attributes
- Creature Families
- Specific named creatures
- Position relationships

Example effects include:

- `+1 if adjacent to a creature with Flight.`
- `+2 if opposite a creature with Magic.`
- `+3 if before another Dragonoid.`
- `+6 if before Phoenix.`
- `-1 if after a Monster.`

### Multiple Ability Effects

If a creature lists multiple ability effects, evaluate each effect independently.

Apply every effect whose condition is satisfied at the time the creature is scored, including both bonuses and penalties.

### Ability Information

Abilities reference information printed on Creature Cards. A creature's family, attributes, identity, and position may therefore affect another creature's score.

### Timing

Ability conditions are checked when the newly placed creature is scored.

Later placements do not change the recorded score of an earlier creature.

---

## 11. Scoring

Each creature is scored individually when played.

The general scoring formula is:

`Final Creature Score = Base Points + Home/Roaming Bonus + Positive Ability Effects - Ability Penalties`

More generally, all signed ability modifiers printed on the card are added to the Base Points and applicable habitat bonus.

### Example

Suppose a creature has:

- Base Points: 4
- Home Habitat: Sky (+2)
- Ability: +2 if opposite a creature with Flight
- Ability: +1 if before a creature with Fortitude

If it is played in Sky and both ability conditions are currently satisfied:

`4 Base + 2 Home + 2 Ability + 1 Ability = 9 points`

Record 9 points for that creature.

### No Retroactive Scoring

Once a creature's score has been calculated and recorded, it remains unchanged.

A creature placed later may use an earlier creature to satisfy its own ability, but the later placement does not cause the earlier creature to be rescored.

---

## 12. Creature Icon Cards / Tokens

Creature Icon Cards / Tokens occupy the physical spaces in a sanctuary.

The full Creature Card remains beside the sanctuary so players can read its:

- Family
- Attributes
- Habitat information
- Ability information

The position of the corresponding icon/token determines that creature's positional relationships within the sanctuary.

---

## 13. End of the Game

The game ends when **each player has played all six Creature Cards from their hand**.

Each player therefore plays six creatures during the game.

At the end of the game:

1. Total each player's recorded creature scores.
2. The player with the highest total score wins.

---

## 14. Important Rules Clarifications

### Does the game end when all six sanctuaries are full?

No. The authoritative end condition is that **every player has played all six cards**.

### Is Adjacent different from Before and After?

Adjacent is the broader relationship. A creature is adjacent if it is either immediately Before or immediately After another creature.

### What direction is Before?

Before is **counter-clockwise**.

### What direction is After?

After is **clockwise**.

### Can a creature be played in any sanctuary?

No. It may only be played in its Home Habitat or one of its listed Roaming Habitats.

### Are creatures rescored after another creature is placed?

No. Scoring uses the board state at the moment the creature is played. Scores are not retroactively recalculated.

### Do attributes automatically score points?

No. Attributes matter only when referenced by an ability.

### Can an empty position satisfy an ability?

No. An ability requiring a creature, family, attribute, or named creature requires an actual creature occupying the relevant position.

### Can multiple ability effects score at once?

Yes. Evaluate each printed effect independently and apply all effects whose conditions are satisfied.

### Does a Roaming Habitat always give +1?

No. Use the exact bonus printed for that specific Roaming Habitat. A Roaming bonus may be positive or zero.

### What happens if the die selects a sanctuary without enough room for all players?

Reroll until a sanctuary with enough empty spaces for every player is selected.

## 15. Canonical Terminology

Use these terms when answering rules questions:

- **Creature Card** — full card containing the creature's rules and statistics.
- **Creature Icon Card / Token** — smaller representation placed into a sanctuary position.
- **Sanctuary** — one of the six habitat areas on the board.
- **Active Sanctuary** — sanctuary selected by the die for the current round.
- **Home Habitat** — creature's primary habitat and associated printed bonus.
- **Roaming Habitat** — alternate legal habitat and its associated printed bonus.
- **Base Points** — starting point value of a creature.
- **Attribute** — Might, Magic, Flight, Mysticism, Fortitude, or Cunning.
- **Ability** — creature-specific scoring condition(s).
- **Adjacent** — immediately Before or After.
- **Before** — immediately counter-clockwise.
- **After** — immediately clockwise.
- **Opposite** — directly across the six-position sanctuary.

---

## 16. Core Rules Summary for Question Answering

When answering a gameplay question, apply these rules in order:

1. Determine the active sanctuary.
2. Confirm the sanctuary has enough empty positions for all players in the round.
3. Confirm the chosen creature lists that sanctuary as Home or Roaming.
4. Determine the creature's selected position.
5. Start with Base Points.
6. Add the printed Home or applicable Roaming bonus.
7. Evaluate every ability effect using the board state at that moment.
8. Interpret Adjacent as Before OR After.
9. Interpret Before as counter-clockwise and After as clockwise.
10. Apply all qualifying positive and negative ability modifiers.
11. Record the score permanently; do not rescore it after later placements.
12. Continue until every player has played all six cards.
13. Highest total recorded score wins.
